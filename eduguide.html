EduGuide AI Tutor

How would a school decide whether an AI tutor is ready for a supervised pilot?

01 / METHODOLOGY

From scope to decision

  1. Map the classroom workflow

    Scope a fictional AI tutor that generates hints and feedback. Identify who is affected and where teachers need to intervene.

  2. Identify student risks

    Map risks such as inaccurate feedback, unequal performance and exposure of student data. Define test scenarios, control owners and escalation routes.

  3. Define the pilot conditions

    Prepare a conditional pilot recommendation based on evidence from representative test scenarios and teacher review.
