AI-powered technical helpdesk

Where could AI reduce repetitive support work while keeping engineers in control?

01 / METHODOLOGY

From scope to decision

  1. Baseline the support queue

    Map triage, knowledge lookup and response drafting. Use a baseline of queue volumes, resolution times and rework when data is available.

  2. Design engineer review

    Start with suggested categories and draft replies. Keep engineers responsible for approval and route sensitive or uncertain tickets for manual review.

  3. Plan a limited pilot

    Design a limited pilot with quality checks, a fallback process and success criteria agreed before launch.
