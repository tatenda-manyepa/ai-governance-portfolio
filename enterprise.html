Enterprise AI adoption

How should an organisation decide which AI use cases to fund, govern and scale?

01 / METHODOLOGY

From scope to decision

  1. Agree access and scope

    Secure permission for interviews and access to internal material. Agree the study scope and what can be shared publicly.

  2. Prioritise use cases

    Assess readiness across data, people, governance and delivery. Compare candidate use cases by value, feasibility and risk.

  3. Sequence investment

    Build a phased roadmap with accountable owners and evidence requirements for each investment decision.
