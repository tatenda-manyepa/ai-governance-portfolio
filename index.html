02 / BACKGROUND

From IT support to connected systems and robotics

My career has taken me from the service desk to customer delivery and then to operations where a software fault can stop physical work. Each role taught me something different about making change work in practice.

IBM Lead AI Transformation: Strategy, Governance & Execution In progressAWS AI Business Strategist In progressMSc Information and Communication TechnologyBSc Biomedical Science

2021–2022 · IT Helpdesk Analyst · Close Brothers

Start with the person, listen to understand

In financial services IT support, I asked what users were trying to do, where they got stuck and what the issue meant for their work. I documented recurring issues and escalated complex cases with evidence. I bring those habits to AI and transformation work: define the problem with the people who experience it, identify what is worth fixing and decide when an issue needs human judgement.

2022–2023 · Associate Technical Consultant · Synamedia

Make the pieces work together

I worked with customers to understand their requirements and presented technical solutions to stakeholders. I helped configure and test broadcast platforms, supported a cloud migration and wrote Python scripts to automate repetitive engineering tasks. I learned to judge improvements by how well they fit the customer’s workflow and to test changes across the whole system.

2024–present · Technical Engineer · Inteq

Assess risk before changing a live system

Supporting warehouse and robotics automation taught me to judge a change by how it affects live operations. I bring that same judgement to AI initiatives. Is AI the right tool? What will it cost, and what value should it deliver? How will we govern it and keep it working? That last question means knowing who owns the change, what evidence shows it worked and how the team can recover if it fails. It also means protecting each layer, from data and applications to the connections between them, and monitoring the system after rollout to spot problems early.
